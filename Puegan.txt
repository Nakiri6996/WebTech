+<body>
+    The b tag means <b>Bold Text</b> - it is used to draw arrention to text without implying any extra imporance or emphasis
+    <p></p>
+    The strong tag means <strong>Strong Importance</strong> - it indicates that the text has high relevance, urgency, or importance.
+    <p></p>
+    The i tag means <i>Italic Text</i> - it represents text in an alternate voice or mood, such as technical terms, idioms, or thoughts.
+    <p></p>
+    The em tag means <em>Emphasized Text</em> - it means text that has stressed emphasis, which changes the meaning of the sentence when spoken aloud.
+    <p></p>
+    The u tag means <u>Underlined Text</u> - it represents text with a non-textual annotation.
+    <p></p>
+    The mark tag means <mark>Highlighted Text</mark> - it marks the text that has been hoghlighted or marked for reference purposes due to its relevance in a specific context.
+    <p></p>
+    The small tag means <small>Small Text</small> - it renders text one size smaller.
+    <p></p>
+    The del tag means <del>Deleted Text</del> - it represents text that has been deleted or removed from the document.
+    <p></p>
+    The ins tag means <ins>Insterted Text</ins> - it represents text that has been added or inserted into the document.
+    <p></p>
+    The sub tag means <sub>Subscript Text</sub> - it renders text half a character height lower than the normal line.
+    <p></p>
+    The sup tag means <sup>Superscript Text</sup> - it renders text half acharacter height higher than the normal line.

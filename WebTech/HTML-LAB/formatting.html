Lab Activity 2: HTML Text Formatting

The b tag means Bold Text - it is used to draw arrention to text without implying any extra imporance or emphasis

The strong tag means Strong Importance - it indicates that the text has high relevance, urgency, or importance.

The i tag means Italic Text - it represents text in an alternate voice or mood, such as technical terms, idioms, or thoughts.

The em tag means Emphasized Text - it means text that has stressed emphasis, which changes the meaning of the sentence when spoken aloud.

The u tag means Underlined Text - it represents text with a non-textual annotation.

The mark tag means Highlighted Text - it marks the text that has been hoghlighted or marked for reference purposes due to its relevance in a specific context.

The small tag means Small Text - it renders text one size smaller.

The del tag means Deleted Text - it represents text that has been deleted or removed from the document.

The ins tag means Insterted Text - it represents text that has been added or inserted into the document.

The sub tag means Subscript Text - it renders text half a character height lower than the normal line.

The sup tag means Superscript Text - it renders text half acharacter height higher than the normal line.
